About Me

My name is Isabella Contreras, and I am a psychology student at the University of California, Riverside. I am interested in understanding human behavior, mental health, and the experiences that shape people's lives. Studying psychology allows me to learn more about how people think, feel, and interact with the world around them.

My long-term goal is to pursue a career in clinical psychology. I hope to continue my education after earning my bachelor's degree and eventually work toward becoming a clinical psychologist. I am especially interested in helping people work through emotional challenges, understand themselves better, and improve their overall well-being. I believe that listening, empathy, patience, and understanding are important qualities for someone who wants to work in mental health.

Outside of school, my experience working at Chick-fil-A has helped me develop customer service, communication, teamwork, and time-management skills. Working in a fast-paced environment has taught me the importance of responsibility, staying organized, and treating people with respect.

Volunteering at my local church to help feed people experiencing homelessness has also given me an opportunity to support my community. This experience has helped me appreciate the importance of compassion and helping people who may be going through difficult circumstances. I have also helped tutor younger students, which has allowed me to practice patience and communication.

As I continue my education, I want to learn more about psychological research, counseling approaches, and the different factors that affect mental health. I hope to gain additional experience through classes, volunteer opportunities, and future professional experiences.

Ultimately, I want my future career to allow me to make a positive difference in people's lives. I hope to use what I learn through my education and experiences to become a knowledgeable, compassionate, and supportive mental health professional.
